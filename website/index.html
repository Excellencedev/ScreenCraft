ScreenCraft zooms into what you click and type, smooths the cursor and frames the result. Free, open source, and it never leaves your computer.

macOS and Windows. MIT license. No account, no watermark.

Zooms that follow what you do

Clicks and typing become zoom regions automatically. The camera glides to the cursor and tracks the text caret while you type. Move, resize or delete any of them.

A cursor you can restyle

The system cursor is never baked in, so size, style, smoothing and click effects are all editable after you record. It hides while you type and returns when you move the mouse.

Captions made on your computer

Generate captions with whisper.cpp. The model downloads once; your audio is never uploaded. Style them TikTok-style, edit the text and timing, or export an SRT.

and then it zooms right in

Export without a watermark

H.264 or HEVC MP4, or GIF, rendered locally with hardware encoding. Add a device frame or a gently moving background, then compress if you need a smaller file.

The rest of the editor

Recorder
A display, a window or a dragged area, with camera, microphone and system audio.
Annotations
Text, arrows, boxes, blur, pixelate and spotlight, pinned to the recording or the frame.
Camera layouts
Bubble, large, camera only or side by side. Every block keeps its own settings and eases between them.
Pace and transitions
Speed up idle stretches, ramp speed, freeze a frame, cross-dissolve between clips.
Audio
Mic levelling, music that ducks under your voice, and optional click and key sounds.
Look
Wallpapers with subtle motion, device frames, padding, corners, shadow and any aspect ratio.

Free under the MIT license. No account, no telemetry, no cloud rendering. Everything is recorded, edited and exported on your own machine, by a native Rust core.
