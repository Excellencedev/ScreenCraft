Features

Five things you will actually do

Record, then shape the result in a real timeline editor.

Zooms that direct attention

Clicks and typing generate zoom regions automatically. They glide with spring physics and follow the text caret, so the viewer always sees what you are doing.

  • Auto-generated from clicks and typing
  • Drag, resize, multi-select and group-move
  • Per-region zoom level

A cursor you can restyle

The system cursor is never baked in. Clicks and keys are logged separately and redrawn, so you can change size, style, smoothing and click effects after the fact.

  • Smoothing and motion-blur controls
  • Click ripples and sounds
  • Hides while you type, returns when you move

A real timeline

Split, trim, reorder, speed up idle stretches, add speed ramps, freeze frames and cross-dissolve transitions. Annotations, camera layouts, captions and music live on their own tracks.

  • Idle detection that finds dead air
  • Speed ramps and freezes
  • Timeline zoom and resizable panes

A finished frame

Wallpapers, animated backgrounds, padding, rounded corners, shadows, device frames and any aspect ratio. Save your look as a preset.

  • Laptop, monitor and tablet frames
  • Subtly animated backgrounds
  • Watermark and presets

Export where you want

H.264 or HEVC MP4, or GIF, rendered locally with hardware encoding. Optional compression after export for smaller files.

  • Up to your source resolution at 60 fps
  • No watermark, ever
  • Standalone compressor included
Depth

Good-looking from the first take.

Defaults tuned so a raw recording already looks like a finished demo, then every detail is yours to change.

  • Auto-zoom on clicks and typing
  • Wallpaper, padding, shadow, corner radius
  • Device frames and animated backgrounds
Camera

Your camera, your screen, or both.

Add a webcam bubble, go full camera, or switch layouts mid-recording from the camera track. Every block has its own settings, and changes ease smoothly.

  • Bubble, large, camera only, side-by-side
  • Nine anchor points, flush to the edge
  • Independent settings per block
Everything else

Recorder, annotations, captions, audio

●

Native recorder

Display, window or dragged area, with microphone, system audio and camera, using each OS's own capture APIs.

✎

Annotations

Text, arrows, boxes, highlight, blur, pixelate and spotlight, pinned to the recording or the frame.

❝

Local captions

On-device transcription powered by whisper.cpp, with TikTok-style animated subtitles and SRT import/export.

♪

Audio tools

Music with fades and ducking under speech, mic levelling, click and key sounds, hold-Space 2× playback.

⌘

Shortcut display

Show shortcuts and special keys on screen. Ordinary typing is never stored.

⏩

Pace & transitions

Speed-up idle gaps, ramps, freeze frames and a true cross-dissolve.
