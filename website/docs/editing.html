The editor

The preview sits above a single timeline. Everything you change is held in memory until you save the project (an asterisk in the title shows unsaved changes).

Timeline

  • Split / trim / reorder clips; delete the parts you don't want.
  • Pace tools: find idle gaps and speed them up, add speed ramps, freeze frames.
  • Transitions: a true cross-dissolve between clips.
  • Timeline zoom and resizable panes for precise work.

Tracks

Zoom, camera layout, annotations (notes), captions and music each have their own track. Drag blocks to move, drag edges to resize, and multi-select several zooms or captions to edit them together.

Style

Backgrounds (wallpapers with optional subtle motion, drift, bokeh, waves or dust, gradients, colours or your own image), device frames (laptop, monitor, tablet, phone or browser window), padding, corner radius, shadow, aspect ratio and a watermark. Save the whole look as a preset.

Audio

Mic levelling, music with fades and automatic ducking under speech, and optional click and key sounds.
