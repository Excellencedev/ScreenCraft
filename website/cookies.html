Cookie Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

This website sets no cookies, so there is no cookie banner.

What is stored in your browser

If you change the theme (light or dark) or the language, the site remembers your choice in your browser's local storage under the keys sc-theme and sc-lang. This is a preference you asked for, it stays on your device, and it is never sent to us or anyone else. Clearing your site data removes it.

Analytics

Traffic statistics come from Cloudflare Web Analytics, which measures visits without cookies or local storage and without tracking individuals. See the privacy policy.

The ScreenCraft desktop app does not use cookies.
